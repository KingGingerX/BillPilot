FAQ

Common questions.

How do I access the dashboard after paying? ▾
After checkout, you'll receive a license key by email. Go to /login, enter your key, and you're in.
Do I need to run ReferIt on my own computer? ▾
ReferIt runs on any machine — your PC, a VPS, or a cloud server. For 24/7 operation, a $5/month VPS (DigitalOcean, Vultr) works perfectly.
Where do I get residential proxies? ▾
We recommend Smartproxy or IPRoyal. A 5GB plan is plenty to start. Configure proxy credentials directly in the dashboard.
Will my real IP ever be exposed? ▾
No. Nextdoor only ever sees your residential proxy IP — a real home internet connection, never your personal or server IP.
Can I cancel at any time? ▾
Yes. Cancel anytime from your Stripe billing portal. No contracts, no questions asked.
