Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need accounts on all platforms?

Yes. AFF LAUNCHER automates product creation, but you still need vendor accounts (and KYC/tax setup) on each marketplace.

Is this a SaaS or a desktop tool?

It is a local Python CLI. Your credentials stay encrypted on your machine. No cloud dependencies required.

What if a platform changes its UI?

Playwright-based platforms use self-healing selector fallbacks. We release updates within 48 hours of marketplace changes.
